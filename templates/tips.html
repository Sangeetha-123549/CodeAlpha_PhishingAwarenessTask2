🛡️ Cyber Security Tips

📧 Verify Email Senders

Always check the sender's email address before opening links or attachments.

🔗 Check URLs Carefully

Hover over links to verify the website address before clicking.

🔒 Use Strong Passwords

Create passwords with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters.

🛡️ Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Add an extra layer of security to your online accounts.

⚠️ Avoid Suspicious Attachments

Never open unexpected attachments from unknown sources.

🔄 Keep Software Updated

Install security updates regularly to protect against threats.
