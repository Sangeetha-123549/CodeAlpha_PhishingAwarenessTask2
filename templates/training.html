Cyber Security Awareness Training

📧 Phishing Emails

Never trust emails asking for passwords, OTPs, or banking details.

🔗 Fake Links

Always verify URLs before clicking. Attackers often use look-alike domains.

🔒 Password Security

Use strong unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication.

🦠 Malware Protection

Do not download files from unknown websites or suspicious emails.
